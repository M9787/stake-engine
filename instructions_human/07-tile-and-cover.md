# 07 — Game tile (3:4) and cover art (16:9) (45 min)

**Why:** Submission is blocked without both. The tile is the first thing players see in the lobby, so it decides clicks. Rules: https://stake-engine.com/docs/approval-guidelines/game-tile-requirements (2026-10-28).

## Steps
1. Open https://studio.engine.io/teams/dumalex/games/oracle-of-sevens/media.
2. Click **Design tile** (3:4). Use the Media Editor:
   - Background: `art_out\tile_bg.png` from the agent.
   - Foreground: `art_out\tile_fg.png` (the main figure, **big**, filling most of the tile).
   - Title: the logo art, inside the safe area.
3. Click **Design cover art** (16:9). Do the same with the 16:9 files.
4. Rules:
   - bright, no dark edges;
   - **no words or numbers** other than the title (no "77,777x", "NEW", "BONUS");
   - the main figure is large.
5. Save both and set the 3:4 tile as the cover.

## Done when
- The Readiness page shows Game tile (3:4) and Cover art (16:9) as done.
