# 08 — Test on your own phone (20 min)

**Why:** Reviewers test on mobile. 3★ needs "tested on a range of devices". The agent can only emulate a phone; you have a real one.

## Steps
1. In Studio, open the game with **Play Game** on your phone. You can also send the link to yourself.
2. Check these 5 things:
   1. **Double-tap** on the game: the page must NOT zoom.
   2. Turn the phone sideways and back: the game still fits, with nothing cut off.
   3. Tap spin, bet +/−, menu, rules and sound: each one works on the first tap.
   4. Play 20 spins: no lag and no sound stutter.
   5. Open a bonus (buy the Star bonus in demo or staging): the animation is smooth.
3. Take a screenshot of anything wrong. Send it to the agent with the phone model.

## Done when
- All 5 checks pass, or the agent has your screenshots.
