# 10 — Handle reviewer feedback

**Why:**
- Three reviewers rate within 24 hours, then one reviewer works through issues with you.
- You get an email for every comment.
- 6 of 9 points or more means the game goes live within 24 hours. Below that, the thread locks for 3 days.

## Steps
1. When an email arrives, open the approval thread in Studio.
2. Copy each comment, word for word, to the agent.
3. The agent fixes the issue and makes a new build. You do task 06 again (upload + publish).
4. Reply in the thread with what was fixed. The agent drafts the reply; you send it.
5. If the game fails: wait 3 days, ask the agent to address every point, then submit again (task 09).

## Remember
- After approval only **visual** fixes are allowed. Math, modes and mechanics cannot change.
