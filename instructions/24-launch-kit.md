# 24 — Launch kit (going viral)

**Goal:** Launch-day material is ready, so the user can post and pitch in under 1 hour.

**Why:**
- Engine's own advice (https://studio.engine.io/docs/promote, 2026-10-28):
  - build an X following;
  - get players to follow the studio on Stake (launch emails get about a 30% open rate);
  - giveaways (for example Paperclip Gaming);
  - Stake Challenges (ask through a Studio support ticket);
  - streamer revenue-share deals;
  - Watchtowr for Kick streamers;
  - paid promotion at promoteonstake.com.
- Trade press (opinion, 2025–2026): clips of huge multipliers and bonus-buy hits drive discovery. New releases get "max-won" by streamers quickly. Streamers like the max win being reachable from buys.

**Depends on:** 21. Clips can be made from storybook before launch.

## Steps
1. **Clips:** record 6 short videos (9:16 and 16:9, 10–30 s each) from storybook with our art:
   - x1024 grid moment;
   - Tower intro;
   - a long tumble chain;
   - big-win tiers;
   - the max win;
   - Star vs Tower side by side.

   Save them to `launch\clips\`. Label them clearly as demo footage; no fake "real money" claims.
2. **X posts:** write `launch\x-posts.md` with:
   - 10 posts: a teaser countdown, the mechanic explained in 1 image, a dev-diary line, the launch post, and 3 days of follow-ups.
   - Keep to X rules for gambling content: 18+, no "guaranteed win" claims.
3. **Streamer pitch:** write `launch\streamer-pitch.md`. It covers a 5-line DM, the game facts, the max win and buy costs, and the clip links. It offers a revenue share; the user decides the % (human task 11).
4. **Stake Challenges ticket:** write `launch\stake-challenge-ticket.md` as ready text for a Studio support ticket.
5. **Giveaway plan:** write `launch\giveaway.md` with a simple follow-and-retweet giveaway. The prize is paid by the user; the user decides the amount. Check the giveaway site's rules before posting.
6. **Stake follow drive:** a 1-line call to action ("Follow DumaLex on Stake for launch day") in every post.

## Done test
- The `launch\` folder holds 6 clips and 4 text files, ready for human task 11.

## Notes
- The agent writes texts but never posts or sends anything. The user does that.
