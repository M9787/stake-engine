# 11 — Promote the launch (about 1 h per week)

**Why:** Engine says a 3★ rating gets you into the lists, but "players still need a reason to find yours". The New Releases order depends on your team's 30-day rank on Engine Insights. Source: https://studio.engine.io/docs/promote (2026-10-28).

The agent prepares every text and clip in `launch\` (agent task 24). You post and send them.

## One week before launch
1. **X account:** create one for your studio (for example @DumaLexGames). Use the provider logo as the profile picture.
2. Post the teaser clips from `launch\clips\`, one per day, with the texts from `launch\x-posts.md`.
3. Add "18+ | Play responsibly" to the bio.

## Launch day
1. Post the launch post on X with the best clip.
2. Ask your followers to **follow DumaLex on Stake**. Stake emails your followers on each launch, with about a 30% open rate.
3. Open a Studio support ticket for **Stake Challenges**, using the text in `launch\stake-challenge-ticket.md`.

## Week 1–4
1. **Streamers:** contact Watchtowr (named in the Engine docs as helping connect with Kick streamers). Send the pitch in `launch\streamer-pitch.md`.
   - Decide yourself what revenue share you offer. Engine suggests a share makes stronger deals.
2. **Giveaway:** run one through a giveaway site such as Paperclip Gaming (`launch\giveaway.md`). You decide the prize.
3. **Paid promotion (optional):** fill in the form at https://promoteonstake.com. Only do this if you want to spend money.
4. Every week, tell the agent what you saw (X replies, streamer comments) so it can log it (agent task 25).

## Rules
- Never claim players will win. Always add 18+.
- Follow X's rules for gambling content in your country.
